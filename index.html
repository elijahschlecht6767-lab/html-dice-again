How to Play

Pick a wager from 11 to 59.
If you roll a number higher than or equal to your wager, you get as many points as you wagered.
If you roll a number lower than your wager, you lose as many points as you wagered.
Get 250 points or more to win.
Go into debt (the negatives or lower than 11) and you lose.
You start with $22.
